Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Xylotune is a free toy xylophone and song composer, available as a web app and an Android app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

Xylotune does not collect your data. Your songs stay on your device, or in a Drive file you control. Nobody who makes this app ever sees them.

What Xylotune stores, and where

Your songs, lyrics, and sound preference are saved only on your own device: in your browser's local storage on the web, or in the app's private storage on Android. This data never leaves your device unless you turn on Google Drive sync yourself.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Xylotune creates one file in your own Drive, named xylotune-songs.json, and keeps it in sync with your saved songs. This uses Google's drive.file permission, which only ever lets the app see and change files it created itself. It cannot see, read, or change any other file in your Drive.

Signing in is handled entirely by Google. Xylotune never receives or stores your Google password, and never asks for your name, email, or profile information, only permission to read and write that one file.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from within the app. Your songs stay saved on your device either way.

Microphone (web app only)

The web app's "Hum it" feature can turn a hummed tune into notes. If you use it, your recording is processed entirely on your own device, inside your browser. It is never uploaded, sent to a server, or stored anywhere beyond that one transcription.

What Xylotune does not do

  • No accounts, and no password to create or remember
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind
  • No sharing of your data with anyone, for any reason
  • No cookies

Children's privacy

Xylotune does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
