Tune to Xylophone

Speed 100%
1–8 write a note  ·  Enter new line  ·  ⌫ delete a dot, or the note once its dots run out  ·  Space/. add a dot  ·  ⇧Del delete a note and its dots  ·  arrow keys or click to move the cursor
The dots after each note are the gap before the next one (hover for the exact time). Click past the dots to add one, click a dot to cut the gap back to that point. Play bars one after another at the end of the pad and your real timing is recorded automatically, pause more than a beat and a new line starts on its own. Turn on 📝 Lyrics to type or edit the words below a line, then click a note (click more to grow the group) and drag-select the matching word(s) to link them - the linked word lights up when its note plays. Click a linked word to unlink it, Esc to drop an armed note selection.
